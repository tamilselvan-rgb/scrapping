About

WETEX Dubai Expo 2026 will be held at Dubai World Trade Centre in Dubai, United Arab Emirates from 20.10.2026 to 22.10.2026. The event, which will be attended industry professionals, creates opportunities to connect with buyers from all over United Arab Emirates and neighboring countries in the region, to source products, and to make export and import agreements. In addition to the exhibitions, WETEX Dubai Expo 2026 offers a number of networking events, educational sessions, and workshops that provide additional opportunities for education and business development for exhibitors and visitors.
