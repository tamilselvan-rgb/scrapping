Introducing the Education Buildings Ireland Exhibitors

Meet the organisations at the forefront of designing, building and creating high-quality learning environments for all students and learners in Ireland

The exhibition at Education Buildings Ireland is your gateway to the latest innovations in school and campus design, construction and technology. Discover sustainable materials, digital tools and modular solutions that are shaping the future of education infrastructure. Meet leading suppliers, gain insights from experts and engage with decision-makers from the Department of Education and across the sector. Whether you’re planning, upgrading or delivering education projects, the exhibition offers fresh ideas and practical solutions, all in one place.
