Your Guide to Cosmetic, Personal Care & HI&I Formulation

SCS Formulate is the UK’s largest event of its kind, focusing on raw materials, ingredients and formulation services used in creating personal care and cosmetic products.

SCS Formulate is the UK’s largest event of its kind, bringing together suppliers of raw materials, ingredients, formulation services and technologies for the cosmetic, personal care and Household, Industrial & Institutional (HI&I) cleaning industries.

Whether you’re developing the next generation of skincare products, haircare, toiletries or cleaning formulations, the exhibition is your opportunity to discover new ingredients, compare suppliers and explore the latest technologies and services supporting product development.

Why visit the exhibition?

  • Source new ingredients, raw materials and speciality chemicals
  • Compare suppliers and formulation technologies side by side
  • Discover testing, analytical and regulatory services
  • Find solutions for cosmetic, personal care and HI&I cleaning products
  • Speak directly with manufacturers, distributors and technical specialists
  • Build valuable connections across the formulation supply chain
