Futures & Options Expo

Today we’re all adapting to the rapid rate of change while pushing to ensure that the cleared derivatives markets are robust and ready for the future. Expo – FIA’s Futures & Options Expo – is your destination for ideas, information and innovations that matter for you, your firm and your clients.
 
Whether you’re a trader chasing the latest trends affecting the markets, a manager seeking ways to increase operational efficiency and cyber resilience, or a business strategist looking for what’s next in tech, you’ll find your answers at Expo. Designed for the doer, Expo brings you industry experts providing actionable recommendations, an exhibit hall full of cutting-edge technologies, products and solutions, plus numerous opportunities for making meaningful connections. Get what you need at Expo.

Mark your calendar for Expo 2026, taking place 2-4 November at the Sheraton Grand Chicago Riverwalk. The room block closes 9 October. Book your room today!

Contact Toni Vitale Chan, Vice President of Business Development at +1 312.636.2919 to discuss 2026 sponsorship and exhibit opportunities.
